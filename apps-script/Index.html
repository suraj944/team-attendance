How the price is worked out

One rate per caller, per period — —. Nothing is charged for admin logins, and a caller marked inactive stops counting from that day. The cards above are that same rate shown at four common team sizes.

Quarterly saves about 16% against paying monthly; annual saves about 30%. Payment is by UPI or bank transfer against an invoice generated inside the admin panel.

What the caller does

  • Posts login with SM, CP firm, location and branch — all set by the admin, so the caller cannot change them
  • Posts logout with total calls, connected calls, lead of the day, site visits done, tomorrow's visits and visits confirmed week to date
  • Picks their own logout time from a 12-hour dropdown, for when they leave early and post late
  • Gets the ready-made WhatsApp message for the group, and can reopen it if WhatsApp was closed by mistake
  • Sees a reminder after your set time if the logout has not been posted
  • Raises a missed-logout request when a day was skipped

What the admin gets

  • Today tab — who is in, who is late, who has not posted, live
  • Approves or rejects missed-logout requests
  • Adds callers one by one, or uploads the whole list from Excel through a spreadsheet-style grid
  • Downloads the caller list as Excel, filtered by branch or search
  • Deletes a caller, with or without their attendance history
  • Resets any caller's password
  • Moves a caller to another branch, with the full transfer history kept

Reports

  • Attendance report by day, caller, branch or SM
  • Monthly report of calls, connected, leads and visits per caller
  • Any date range you choose
  • Employee Summary and Branch Summary sheets that keep themselves up to date — working days, present, absent, late
  • Branch history of every transfer
  • Everything exports to Excel

Your setup and your data

  • Your own lists — branches, sales managers, locations, CP firms
  • Your own rules — late-after time, logout reminder time, weekly off, the date absence starts counting
  • Optional PAN and Aadhaar against each caller
  • Billing built in — plan, caller count, payment record and printable invoice
  • All of it sits in your own Google Sheet. No other company holds your attendance data, and it stays yours if you ever stop paying
Prices in Indian rupees. Billed to the company, collected by UPI or bank transfer.
Changing plan takes effect from the next period — the current paid period always runs to its end date.
